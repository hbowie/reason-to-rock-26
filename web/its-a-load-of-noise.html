Jagger with Rolling Stone ↑

It’s a load of noise

People say that concert audiences are listening now, but to what? Like the Rolling Stones on stage just isn’t the Boston Pops Symphony Orchestra. It’s a load of noise. On record it can be quite musical but when you get to the stage it’s no virtuoso performance.

Mick Jagger, from the interview “Jagger with Rolling Stone”
